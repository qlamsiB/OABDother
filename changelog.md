# new

- **NEW STAND: GREEN DAY**
  - **Passive: Mold Stacking**
    - Upon mold being applied to an opponent, it will add a stack, mold deals damage overtime with more stacks dealing more damage but dissipating quicker with exponential increase in damage and exponential decrease in duration, damage also increases based on Green Day users height. When mold kills someone, it creates a mold spreading corpse.
  - **Passive: Amputation**
    - The user possessing the Green Day stand can amputate parts of their body, amputations will inflict self damage that can be healed from the Restitch move.
  - **E - Burst Barrage**
    - Green Day performs a powerful, armored barrage that cannot be canceled.
  - **R - Arm Chop**
    - Green Day swings a chop that severs enemy arms, can be used for Restitch.
  - **T (Hold) - Fumigate**
    - Green Day spreads mold in-front, inflicting mold stacks. Slows down the user while being held.
  - **Y - Corpse Carry**
    - The user can pick up and throw mold corpses. Can be used during Stand moves.
  - **F - Moldboom**
    - The user and Green Day strike a pose before exploding with a burst of mold, inflicting nearby opponents with stacks of mold inversely proportional to their distance to the user.
  - **G - Detatchable Hands**
    - If the left arm is not detatched, the user will cut it off themselves. When cut off, this move will shoot the arm towards where the cursor is aimed, dealing damage to people struck by it. Can be used during Stand moves.
  - **H - Sever**
    - The user cuts themselves in half and ejects their upper torso forward, this places them in a crawling state that lessens jump strength but can be used during stand moves to get out of situations. When used while detatched, the player's lower half will jolt towards the player's current position, dealing damage to people hit by it.
- **V - Restitch**
    - The user will add any limbs that are on the ground with themselves that they themselves are missing, healing them.
- **B - Incision Slash**
    - The user swings a scalpel forth, dealing small damage and berserking. This move can be used with other stand moves.
- It is obtained from arrows at a normal chance.
